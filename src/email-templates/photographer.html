Welcome to LapSnaps – The Track & Drag Registry/Marketplace!

We’re excited to have you on board as one of our first photographers.

📌 Set up your shop profile

  • Upload your profile picture and banner image in Settings
  • Use lower resolution images for faster loading
  • Choose a wide banner image for better display

⚙️ Choose your defaults

  • Select your default currency and default price per photo
  • These auto-fill during uploads (editable per photo)

📂 Add new media

Go to Media Files → + Add Media and fill in details:

  • Location (e.g. Bahrain International Circuit)
  • Date Captured
  • Price & Currency
  • Car Registration / Vehicle Make & Model
  • For mixed photos, enable “Multiple vehicles”

⬆️ Upload your images/videos

  • Drag & drop or click to select files
  • Click Upload Photos when ready
  • Large high-res uploads may take longer (watermarks applied automatically)

🛠 Manage your media

Your uploads appear in Media Files — preview, edit, or delete anytime.

📢 Share your shop

  • Photos automatically display in your Shop
  • Your custom QR code is in the shop corner
  • Print it or share it with car owners for instant access

📱 Promote on social media

  • Post the same day you photograph to boost sales
  • Share a picture, tag the location, and link your LapSnaps shop

🎉 That’s it! Your media is now protected and ready for owners to browse.

Need help? Email us at info@lapsnaps.com.

Thanks for joining us. Let’s capture the track, one LapSnap at a time.
